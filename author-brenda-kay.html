Why the FreeIQ Sites Exist

The tools that help people make real financial decisions — mortgage calculators, insurance estimators, legal cost guides — are almost always buried behind email forms, lead-gen funnels, or paywalls designed to connect you with a salesperson rather than answer your question.

Brenda built FreeMoneyIQ, FreeInsuranceIQ, and FreeLegalIQ to give people the number first — so they can walk into any financial conversation informed, not surprised. Every calculator runs in your browser, returns an honest estimate in seconds, and asks for nothing in return.

She writes and reviews all editorial content across the three sites, drawing on her own experience navigating the same financial questions the tools are designed to answer.
